Net metering is a billing mechanism that credits solar energy system owners for the electricity they add to the grid. During midday when your solar panels generate more electricity than your home consumes, your electric meter runs backwards, generating credits to offset the electricity you draw at night.

Our calculator divides your annual electricity consumption (kWh) by the annual solar output expected from 1 kW of solar capacity in your climate (calculated as daily peak sun hours × 365 × a standard 78% system performance ratio), factoring in inverter and temperature deratings.
