Step-by-Step Blueprint

Solar Panel Installation Guide

A comprehensive 5-phase roadmap for community homeowners and neighborhood associations to successfully execute a solar project.

Phase 1
Energy Audit & Sizing

Review past 12 months of utility electric bills to determine total annual kWh demand and analyze roof azimuth and shading.

  • Determine peak monthly kWh consumption
  • Satellite roof LiDAR tilt & orientation audit
  • Check electrical main service panel (200A)
Phase 2
Quotations & System Design

Receive multiple bids from certified NABCEP installers. Compare monocrystalline bifacial panels and microinverters vs string systems.

  • 3 competitive contractor proposals
  • Review 25-year manufacturer warranties
  • Incorporate optional LFP battery storage
Phase 3
Permitting & Interconnect

Submit engineering line diagrams and structural calculations to the local municipal building department and electric utility.

  • Local municipal electrical building permit
  • HOA or neighborhood association approval
  • Utility Net Metering Interconnect Agreement
Phase 4
Physical Installation & Wiring

Certified technicians mount heavy-duty aluminum racking into rafters, lay flashing to prevent roof leaks, and securely fasten solar modules.

  • Lag bolts and weatherproof waterproof flashing
  • DC conduit routing and inverter grounding
  • Rapid shutdown switch and AC disconnect safety switch
Phase 5
Inspection & Permission to Operate (PTO)

City inspector reviews physical build against the permit. Utility installs a bi-directional smart meter and issues PTO.

  • Municipal safety and code sign-off
  • Bi-directional net meter installation by utility
  • Mobile app monitoring telemetry activated!
